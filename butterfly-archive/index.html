1. The Butterfly Archive

    Lepidoptera matrilinea: one wing from each generation.

    Each specimen in this collection was mounted by a visitor. The left wing is half of their mother's face, and the right wing is half of their own. The two remaining halves are laid over one another to form the body.

    Mount your own specimen

    Scan with your phone camera. You will need a photograph of yourself and one of your mother.
